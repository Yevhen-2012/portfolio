Lumora

Lumora is a demonstration project for an online store offering decorative and scented candles. Created for a portfolio, it explores product presentation, catalogue structure, and the visual identity of a retail website.

Webflow Development, CMS & E-commerce

Tools

Figma, Webflow, Photoshop

Behind the Build

Role
Work on the Lumora demonstration website and its portfolio presentation. This case study focuses on page composition, visual hierarchy, and the presentation of collections and individual products.
‍
Constraints
The project was created for demonstration. The case study discusses visual decisions without claiming sales results or conversion improvements.
‍
Solution
The design uses warm neutral colours, large product photographs, and contrasting typography. The opening screen presents sculptural candles as objects within an interior. Each collection has a distinct visual setting, ranging from bright Mediterranean scenes to dark wood and warm firelight. The catalogue uses restrained product cards with a name, collection label, and price. A Journal section extends the store’s content with stories about candles, craft, and the atmosphere of a space.
‍
Process
The case study presents the work through four main areas: the opening screen, collection features, the product catalogue, and Journal. Each area serves a different purpose. The opening screen draws attention through photography and oversized type. Collection features group products by mood, the catalogue supports comparison, and Journal pairs imagery with editorial content.

From Vision to Result

The project brings together a consistent visual concept for the opening screen, collections, product catalogue, and Journal.It demonstrates an approach to presenting a product range: photography creates the first impression, while a consistent structure helps visitors move towards individual products.
