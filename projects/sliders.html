Sliders

These slider concepts explore two approaches to presenting visual content through motion. One uses a structured grid, while the other creates a more fluid, layered sequence. Thoughtful transitions and responsive behavior keep each interaction clear, engaging, and easy to navigate across devices.

Behind the Build

Two slider concepts were designed and built as focused experiments in motion, layout, and user interaction. Each version combines a clear visual structure with smooth GSAP animation and responsive Webflow development. The goal was to make every transition feel precise, natural, and visually connected to the content across all screen sizes.

  1. Prepare:  Define the visual direction, slide structure, interaction logic, and animation timing.
  2. Build: Develop responsive layouts in Webflow and integrate the required custom functionality.
  3. Animate: Create GSAP transitions, refine motion details, and test performance across different devices.

The Result

The result is a pair of responsive sliders that turn static content into a clear, engaging visual experience. Smooth GSAP transitions, balanced pacing, and precise interaction details keep each movement natural and easy to follow. Both concepts adapt seamlessly across screen sizes while maintaining their structure, rhythm, and visual impact.
