Art Studio

Zhaden is a contemporary art studio website built to place each artwork at the center of the experience. A restrained layout, expressive imagery, subtle interactions, and responsive Webflow development create a clear digital space where visitors can explore the studio’s work and visual identity.

Role

Tools

Figma, Webflow, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, Visual Studio Code.

Behind the Build

The Zhaden Art Studio process combined visual design with Webflow development to create a clean, expressive digital space. Typography, imagery, and composition were shaped to keep the artwork in focus, while custom animations added movement without distracting from the studio’s work.

  1. Explore: Define the visual direction and organize artwork, fonts, and reference materials.
  2. Design: Create the layout in Figma and build a clear visual system around the artwork.
  3. Develop: Transfer the design to Webflow, add custom animations, and refine responsiveness across every screen size.

The Result

The result is a bold yet balanced digital experience shaped around Zhaden’s artistic identity. A restrained layout, expressive imagery, and fluid interactions keep the artwork at the center of attention. Every section combines visual character with clear navigation, creating an art studio website that feels modern, distinctive, and easy to explore.
